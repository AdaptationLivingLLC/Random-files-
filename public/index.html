Open a message
  1. Paste the encrypted emoji you received and select Open encrypted emoji.
  2. Choose its matching .vkey key file.
  3. Enter the message passphrase and select Prepare to reveal.
  4. Press and hold on the message area. Move your finger to read through the circle.

The timer starts on your first hold: 10 words gives you 4 seconds. Releasing hides the text but does not pause the timer. Offline packages do not require the daily access code.
